# SkinLedger: demo video script (target 2:30–3:00)

Length rule: 2:30–3:00 on YouTube (unlisted or public), 3:00 hard maximum. The voice-over below is about 370 words (≈ 2:40 at 140 wpm, ≈ 2:50 with pauses over screen action). Read it aloud once with a timer before recording; add pauses if under 2:30, trim if over 2:55. Only Francisco's face, never a family member. No music (silence plus voice-over). No third-party brands or logos in frame. English voice-over, English subtitles.

Only Francisco's face appears. Never a family member, in frame, in a reflection, in the background, in a screenshot, or in a profile name. Never show a mock-up as real.

## Shot list
1. **Mirror opener** (landscape or vertical, 1080p): Francisco's face, natural light, neutral background. 5 s.
2. **Phone screen recordings** (vertical, native screen recording): home, consent, Camera Kit capture, result. Use the real REAL day-1 data.
3. **Camera Kit capture over the shoulder** (optional): phone held in frame so the guided capture is visibly on the target device. Only Francisco's face.
4. **Upload try page**: Francisco's own selfie only.
5. **Noise-band page**: screen capture on laptop or phone, zoom into the pore and acne bars and the "Unstable for your setup" chips.
6. **Product form**: select only stable target aspects and show the greyed-out unstable ones.
7. **Timeline** of the public SIMULATED sample profile. Keep the SAMPLE banner in frame.
8. **Verdicts**: the sample's KEEP with the confounded warning, the sample's No measurable change, and Francisco's own TOO_EARLY before day 12.
9. **Privacy page and delete**.
10. **Closing card**: text on a plain background.
